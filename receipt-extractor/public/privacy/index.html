Privacy policy

Short version: we read your receipts to extract the fields, then forget them.

Last updated:
October 2026

What we process

When you upload a receipt, the file is sent to our server so the date, vendor, amount, tax, currency and category can be extracted. The text of PDF receipts, or the image itself for photos, is sent to our AI provider (Groq) to do the reading.

What we store

Nothing. Files are processed in memory and discarded when the request ends. The extracted rows live only in your browser until you download them or close the page. We do not keep copies of your receipts or results.

Third parties

  • Groq processes receipt content to extract the fields. See Groq's own privacy policy for how they handle API data.
  • Hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on this website.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email app. We only receive what you choose to send, and use it only to reply to you.

Your choices

Don't upload receipts that contain information you aren't comfortable sharing with the providers above. For any privacy question, email .
