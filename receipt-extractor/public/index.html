§09FAQ

Questions, answered

What files can I upload?

PDF receipts and invoices, plus images in PNG, JPG and WEBP (phone photos and screenshots). You can mix them in one batch of up to 20 files.

What happens when the AI can't read something?

It leaves the field empty and marks the row "needs review" with a note explaining what is missing. It never invents a value. Tax is the only field allowed to be empty, because many receipts don't show it.

Do you store my receipts?

No. Files are processed in memory to extract the fields and are then discarded. We don't keep copies of your receipts or your results.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs are just pictures with no text inside. Upload them as an image (PNG or JPG) instead and the vision model will read them.

Can I import the results into my accounting software?

Yes. Download the CSV and import it into the spreadsheet or accounting tool you already use. The Excel report is best for sharing with people.

Which categories does it use?

Meals, Travel, Office Supplies, Software, Utilities, Groceries, Entertainment, Health and Other. Need your own categories? Talk to us.
