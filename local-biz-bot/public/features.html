Features

Accurate answers, honest hand-offs, real leads.

Local Biz Bot is built around one rule: it only tells your customers what your own website says. Here's everything that makes that work.

Learns your business from your website

Enter your website address and Local Biz Bot finds your pages through your sitemap (or the links on your homepage), reads up to 30 pages, and removes menus and scripts to keep the readable text.

The text is split into small chunks and indexed by meaning, so a question like "do you fix leaky taps?" finds the page about faucet repair even if the words don't match.

A business profile with your key facts

Phone numbers, hours and prices are what customers ask most. Instead of hoping search finds them, Local Biz Bot extracts them once into a structured profile and uses it for every answer.

You can see the extracted profile in your dashboard and check it's correct.

Knows whether you're open right now

AI models are unreliable with dates and time zones. So Local Biz Bot works out "open or closed" in code, using your hours and your local time zone, and gives the AI a ready-made answer.

Are you open right now?
We're closed right now. We open today at 8:00 and close at 18:00.

Never makes up a price

The assistant may only quote a price that is written on your website, exactly as written. If a customer asks about a job you haven't priced online, it says so and offers a call back.

Your website text is treated as information, never as instructions, which protects against people trying to trick the bot.

How much to replace a kitchen sink?
I don't have a price for that. Please call (512) 555-0147, or leave your number for a call back.

Turns unanswered questions into leads

When the assistant hands over, a small form asks for the visitor's name and phone or email. Their request, including the question they asked, lands in your dashboard.

One line of code, works anywhere

The chat bubble is a single small script with no libraries. It runs in an isolated Shadow DOM, so it never clashes with your website's design.

You can set the title, greeting and brand color with simple attributes.

<script src="https://localbizbot.app/static/widget.js" data-site="brightside-plumbing-com" data-color="#0f766e" defer></script>
