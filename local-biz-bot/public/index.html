FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Do I need to write FAQs or train the bot?

No. Local Biz Bot reads your existing website. If the answer is on your site, the bot can find it. When your website changes, refresh it from the dashboard and the bot re-reads your pages.

What happens if a customer asks something that isn't on my website?

The bot says politely that it doesn't have that information, gives your phone number if your site lists one, and shows a short call-back form. The request appears as a lead in your dashboard.

Which website builders does it work with?

Any website where you can add a script tag: WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify, Webflow, Wix (on plans that allow custom code) and hand-written HTML sites.

Will the chat bubble break my website's design?

No. The widget lives in an isolated Shadow DOM, so your site's styles can't change it and its styles can't affect your site. It's one small script with no libraries.

Can someone copy my snippet onto another website?

It won't work for them. Each assistant only answers requests that come from your own domain.

Does it store my visitors' conversations?

No. Chat messages are answered and not saved. The only visitor data stored is what someone types into the call-back form: name, phone or email, and the question they asked.
