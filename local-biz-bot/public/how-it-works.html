How it works

From your website address to a working assistant.

Two parts: a one-time setup that reads your website, and the chat that answers every visitor question. Here's what happens in each.

Part 1 · Setup

Reading your website

Done once, from the owner dashboard. Run it again any time your website changes.

1

Find your pages

Local Biz Bot looks for your sitemap.xml (including WordPress sitemaps). If there isn't one, it follows the links on your homepage.

2

Read and clean

It downloads up to 30 pages from your domain and keeps the readable text, removing menus, scripts and styling. Phone and email links are collected as reliable contact details.

3

Build your business profile

Your homepage and your contact, about, services and pricing pages are read by an AI model that fills in a fixed profile: name, phone, email, address, time zone, services, service areas, hours and prices. It's told never to guess.

4

Index everything else

All page text is split into small chunks and stored in a vector database, searchable by meaning. Repeated headers and footers are stored only once.

Part 2 · Every question

Answering a visitor

Each question gets a fresh, grounded answer in a second or two.

1

Check the website

The request must come from the business's own domain. Requests from anywhere else are blocked.

2

Gather the facts

The assistant gets three things: the business profile, an "open or closed right now" sentence calculated in code, and the four website chunks closest in meaning to the question.

3

Answer, or hand over

The AI answers in one to three sentences using only those facts. If the answer isn't there, it says so and marks the conversation for a hand-over.

4

Capture the lead

On a hand-over, the visitor sees a call-back form. Their name, phone or email and question are saved for the owner.

Under the hood

Simple, proven building blocks

No heavy frameworks. Every part does one job.

FastAPI

The Python web API for the chat, leads and the owner dashboard.

Groq LLM

A fast large language model that writes the answers and extracts the profile, returning strict JSON.

Qdrant

Vector database holding page chunks, business profiles and leads, separated per business.

FastEmbed

Turns text into vectors (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) so questions match pages by meaning.

Installing

Where to paste the code

Paste the snippet from your dashboard just before the closing </body> tag of your site.

WordPress

Use a header and footer plugin (such as WPCode) and paste the snippet into the footer section.

Squarespace, Webflow, Shopify

Paste it into the site-wide footer code injection or theme.liquid, before </body>.

Custom HTML

Add it before </body> on every page, or in your shared footer template.
