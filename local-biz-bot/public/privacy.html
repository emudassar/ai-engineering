Privacy

What we store, and what we don't.

Plain language, no surprises.

Website content

During setup, Local Biz Bot reads public pages of the business website and stores their text and an extracted business profile (name, contact details, hours, services, service areas and prices) so it can answer questions.

Chat messages

Visitor questions are sent to an AI model to write an answer. They are not saved in our database.

Call-back requests

If a visitor fills in the call-back form or the contact form, we store their name, phone number or email, and their question, so the business owner can reply. Only the owner can see these, behind a password.

Cookies

This website and the chat widget do not set tracking cookies.

Questions

For any privacy question or to delete your data, use the contact form.
