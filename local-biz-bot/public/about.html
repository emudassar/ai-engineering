About

AI that small businesses can actually trust.

Local Biz Bot started with a simple observation: local businesses lose customers to unanswered questions, and most chatbots make the problem worse by guessing.

Our story

Why we built it

A plumber on a job can't answer the phone. A dentist's front desk closes at five. But their websites are open all night, full of answers nobody can find quickly.

Off-the-shelf AI chatbots promised to help, but they made up prices and opening hours. For a small business, one wrong price can cost a customer and a review.

So we built an assistant with strict rules: answer only from the business's own website, calculate times in code, never invent a price, and hand over to a human when unsure.

Our principles

  • Accuracy over cleverness. A short correct answer beats a long confident guess.
  • Honest hand-offs. "I don't know, here's how to reach us" is a good answer.
  • Respect for privacy. Chat messages aren't stored. Only call-back requests are.
  • Simple by design. One line of code, no training, no maintenance.
